Selected work
01
Mode Designs · Software Engineering + Workflow Research

Building a Fulfillment Tool for Complex Product Configurations

Problem: Fulfillment employees had to navigate complex product configurations and dependencies when assembling orders.

Outcome: Built a custom Shopify application that translated product configurations into actionable picklists and station assignments, reducing packing time by 30%.

02
Mode Designs · Software Engineering + Product Problem-Solving

Making Customer Images Discoverable for Configurable Products

Problem: Real-world photographs of configurable keyboards were scattered across internal and external sources, making them difficult to organize and display.

Outcome: Built a full-stack image management system for tagging, searching, and displaying approximately 500 customer and community photographs on Shopify product pages.

03
Mode Designs · LLM Prototyping + Customer Research

Prototyping an AI-Powered Customer Research Assistant

Problem: Finding patterns in open-ended customer survey responses required manually reviewing and organizing feedback.

Outcome: Developed an internal prototype using LlamaIndex and OpenAI to support natural-language exploration of customer feedback.

About

My interest in the human mind has taken me through psychology, neuroscience, and computer science. I'm fascinated by how people think and behave, how our experiences and environments shape us, and how we can use what we learn to create things that are genuinely useful.

My experience spans academic and clinical research, behavioral health, and software engineering. I've enjoyed approaching problems from different angles, whether that's investigating a research question, understanding how people interact with a system, or building a tool to solve a practical problem. I'm particularly interested in work that brings together technology, human behavior, and thoughtful design.
